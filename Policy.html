Privacy Policy
Introduction
Welcome to Disguise Clothing ! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website http://disguiseclothing.in or make a purchase.
Information We Collect
A.) Personal Information: We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you place an order or contact us.
B.) Payment Information: Payment details are processed through a secure payment gateway and are not stored on our servers.
C.) Usage Data: We may collect data on how you interact with our website, including IP address, browser type, and pages visited.
How We Use Your Information
• Process and fulfill your orders
• Communicate with you about your orders or inquiries
• Improve our website and customer service
• Send promotional materials (if you opt-in)
Order Process
Order Process at Disguise
Place Your Order
Browse our collection and select your desired items. Add them to your cart and proceed to checkout.
Order Confirmation
Once you place your order, you will receive a confirmation email with your order details.
⚠️ Important: Your order will be confirmed only after payment verification, which will take place within 24 hours of your order being placed.
Processing and Dispatch
After payment confirmation, we will begin processing your order.
This process usually takes 2-3 days before dispatch.
Shipping and Delivery
Once dispatched, your order will be delivered within 7 days, depending on your location. you’ll receive a tracking number so you can monitor your delivery.
Order Completion
Your order is complete once you receive it. Enjoy your new clothing piece from Disguise ! For any queries, feel free to contact our customer support team.
Refund, Return, and Exchange Policy
At DISGUISE, we strive to ensure you love your purchase. However, if you are not fully satisfied with your order, we offer a hassle-free return, refund, or exchange under the conditions below:
Eligibility for Returns, Refunds, or Exchanges:
Video Proof Requirement: To qualify for a return, refund, or exchange, you must provide a clear video of the unpacking of the product upon receipt. The video should:
Show the unsealed package and the product as it is being removed.
Clearly display the condition of the product upon opening, including any defects, damages, or discrepancies.
Request Timeline:
You must initiate a return, refund, or exchange request within 4-7 days of receiving the product.
Condition of the Product:
The item must be unused, unworn, and in the original packaging, with all tags intact.
Non-Eligible Items: Certain products such as undergarments, accessories, and custom-made or personalized items are not eligible for return or exchange.
How to Request a Refund or Exchange:
Step 1: Record a clear video of the unpacking process when you first open the package.
Step 2: Contact our customer support team at clothingdisguise@gmail.com within 3 days of receiving your order, and provide:
The video of the unpacking.
A description of the issue (damaged product, wrong item, etc.).
Step 3: Once your video and request are reviewed, we will provide further instructions for returning the product (if necessary).
Refund Process:
Upon receiving and inspecting the returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to yours original payments methods within 4 – 7 business days.
Exchanges:
Exchanges are subject to product availability. If you wish to exchange an item, please follow the same process for return requests. We will arrange the exchange after verifying the product condition through the provided video and receiving the returned item.
Shipping Fees:
Shipping fees for returns and exchanges are the responsibility of the customer, unless the return is due to a defect or error on our part.
Note:
Failure to provide the required unpacking video will result in the return, refund, or exchange request being denied.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on our website, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at clothingdisguise@gmail.com.
